Privacy

Last updated September 2026.

PyQuest is a free, educational project for learning Python. This page explains, in plain language, what it stores about you, why, and who else sees it.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking or analytics cookies, and your data is never sold.
  • Your code runs in your own browser, not on our server.
  • The AI Coach never receives your name, email address, or password.
  • Guest accounts, and everything in them, are deleted after {{ guest_days }} days.

What we store

  • Your account: display name, email address, timezone, and whether you appear on the leaderboard. Your password is never stored, only a secure, one-way scrambled version of it (a hash).
  • Your learning: code you save and submit, test results, error types, XP, level, streaks, badges, daily missions, hints, messages with the Coach, and your ratings of Coach replies.
  • Administrative records: if an administrator changes your roles or account status, the change is logged, with who made it and when.

We use this only to run PyQuest: to keep you signed in, save your progress, grade your solutions, show your dashboard, and help instructors improve challenges.

Cookies

PyQuest uses one cookie, which keeps you signed in and protects forms from being submitted by other websites. It ends after 30 minutes of inactivity. There are no advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies.

The AI Coach

When you ask for a hint, an explanation, a code review, a progress summary, or chat with the Coach, PyQuest sends Google's Gemini AI service the challenge description, its visible examples, your code, and your message or statistics. It never sends your name, email address, or password, and never the challenge's hidden tests or model answer.

PyQuest uses Gemini's free tier, under which Google may use what is sent to improve its services. Please do not put personal information in your code or chat messages.

Your code

Your Python code runs inside your own browser. The server receives your code (to save it and record your submissions) and the output it produced, but never runs it.

What others can see

The public leaderboard shows your display name, level, and XP, never your email address. You can hide yourself from it at any time in Settings. Instructors can see statistics about how learners do on their challenges, and administrators can see account details to manage the site.

Guests

"Try as Guest" creates a temporary account with no name or email. Guests can send {{ guest_chat_limit }} Coach chat messages. Guest accounts and all their data are deleted after {{ guest_days }} days, unless you create a free account first, which keeps your progress.

Security

PyQuest uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) when deployed, protects every form against forgery, limits repeated attempts to log in or sign up, and signs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity.

Your choices

You can change your display name, timezone, and leaderboard visibility in Settings. To have your account deleted, contact the developer through the project's GitHub page.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top will be updated.
